Core Features

Designed for Speed, Security & Simplicity

Explore the powerful capabilities built directly into the PappyShip application ecosystem.

Local Discovery by ZIP

Easily discover trusted local service businesses right in your area using a simple 5-digit ZIP code. Find exclusive membership options near your home or office without continuous GPS tracking.

Stripe-Powered Billing

Pay with total peace of mind. All financial transactions and recurring billing are handled via Stripe's secure PaymentSheet. PappyShip never stores raw card or CVV details.

Instant QR Code Redemption

Checking in for your service is fast and contactless. Simply show your active subscription QR code; the provider scans it on-device in real time to verify and redeem visits immediately.

Predictable Recurring Revenue

For service providers, PappyShip turns irregular appointments into reliable recurring cash flow. Set up flexible membership tiers and build lasting customer relationships.

Cross-Platform Mobile App

Crafted natively for iOS and Android with modern Flutter architecture. Experience smooth performance, intuitive navigation, and reliable background synchronization.

Customer Control & Privacy

Full subscription autonomy with no hidden fees or locked contracts. Manage renewals, pause memberships, or request account deletion easily with total privacy protection.
